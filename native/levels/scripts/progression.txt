# wing (Prima and rogue.site: Grace takes the Fuse back to the gate and it
# opens). The map's only way west from Room 203's wing is the plain Locked
# Door at 79,107, so that door is the gate and its fuse box sits beside it.

obj fusebox 80 109 name=fusebox

# The F2 south-east stairs drop Grace into a walled landing on floor 1 whose

# through it from the landing (it is how her side of the Care Center joins
# the Elevator corridor and the Medication Room, where the West Wing

# The warped closets stay shut.
